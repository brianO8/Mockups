Employee List

Employee List Guide

The Employee List helps manage employee records and supports scheduling, payroll, and compliance. Most data is imported from your HCM or payroll system.

Note: Make changes in your source system—not in Optim8—to avoid inconsistencies or lost data.

Search, Sort & Export

  • Filter results by division, department, or other options at the top.
  • Click column headers to sort.
  • In Optim8, use page buttons to navigate.
  • Click Report to download full employee data.

Employee Details

  • Click an employee ID to view details.
  • Email and phone are used for portal access and password recovery.

Key Tabs

  • Compliance: Assign rules for minors (auto-assigned if configured).
  • Jobs: View job history, including position, rate, and end dates. If all jobs have an end date, the employee will be marked inactive.
  • Misc: Set time zone and portal clock-in permissions.
  • Availability: Approve, reject, or edit submissions.
  • Certifications: Track required roles.
  • Service Log: Add performance notes.
  • Notification: Show messages at clock-in (must be manually cleared).

Manually Add an Employee

  • Click Add New.
  • Fill in required fields: ID, First/Last Name, DOB, and Org Levels.
  • Email, phone, address, and SSN are optional.
  • Company fills automatically.
